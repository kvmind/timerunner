Privacy Policy

TimeRunner for Mac · Updated 27 November 2026

IN SHORT

TimeRunner does not collect, send or sell any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app itself makes no network connections; the subscription and the purchase are handled by the App Store, which is part of macOS.

WHAT IS KEPT, AND WHERE

Everything you enter — clients and their contact and billing details, projects, tasks, time records, rates, goals, invoices, your business details and logo, focus sessions and settings — is kept in a file on your Mac, in the app's own protected folder, which only TimeRunner and its widget can read. None of it is sent to the developer or to anyone else, and the developer has no copy of it.

BACKUPS

TimeRunner keeps daily backup copies of that file on your Mac. If you choose a backup folder — in iCloud Drive or Dropbox, for instance — copies are written there as well, and the service that folder belongs to then handles them under its own terms.

EXPORTS

Reports and invoices (PDF, Excel, CSV) are saved where you choose, and reach other people only when you send them.

PURCHASES

The free month, the subscription and the lifetime purchase are carried out by the App Store with your Apple Account, under Apple's privacy policy. TimeRunner opens no connection for them: it asks macOS, and macOS tells it only whether this Apple Account has a subscription or a purchase and until when. The developer never sees your name, your email address or your payment details. Apple reports sales to the developer as totals, without anything that identifies you.

AI TOOLS

The MCP connector is off until you switch it on. While it is on, an AI tool that you have connected, running on your Mac, can read your clients, projects, tasks and time records and, if you allow it, add new ones. What that tool does with what it reads — including sending it to its maker's servers — is governed by that tool's own privacy policy, not by this one. The connector can be switched off at any time in Settings → MCP.

APP LOCK

Touch ID and the Mac password are checked by macOS. TimeRunner is told only whether the check passed; it never sees a fingerprint or a password.

NOTIFICATIONS

Notifications are made on your Mac. No push service is involved.

DIAGNOSTICS FROM APPLE

If you have agreed in macOS to share crash and usage data with app developers, Apple may pass on anonymous crash reports and totals such as the number of downloads. The app adds nothing of its own to them.

CHILDREN

TimeRunner is not directed at children, and collects nothing from anyone.

REMOVING YOUR DATA

Delete records in the app, or remove the app together with its data folder: nothing is left anywhere else.

CHANGES AND CONTACT

If this policy changes, the new text is published with the app and at https://kvmind.github.io/timerunner/privacy.html, with its date. Questions go to the support page: https://kvmind.github.io/timerunner/support.html
