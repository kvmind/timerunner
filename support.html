Support

TimeRunner for Mac

A question, a problem or a request? Write it up and it will be answered.

Open a support request

Requests are kept on GitHub and are public: leave out client names and anything else that is private. Earlier ones are listed here.

Where is my data?

In one file on your Mac, in the app's own protected folder. Nothing you enter is sent anywhere, and there is no account. See the privacy policy.

How do I back it up or move to another Mac?

Settings → Backup. The app keeps a copy for each day for about a month, on this Mac and in a folder you choose — iCloud Drive or Dropbox, say. On the other Mac, choose the same folder and restore the latest copy.

How do I add the widget?

Pin a task on its page — Pin to widget, Pin to dashboard — or in Settings → Pinned tasks; then right-click the desktop → Edit Widgets → TimeRunner. The Focus timer has widgets of its own in the same place.

How do I connect an AI tool?

Settings → MCP: switch the connector on and use the button for your tool — Claude, ChatGPT desktop, Codex, Cursor, VS Code and others. The connector is off until you switch it on, and a tool can read and add but never edit or delete.

The app asks for Touch ID. Can I switch that off?

Settings → Access → Biometric lock. It is off unless you switched it on.

Which Macs does it run on?

Apple silicon and Intel, macOS 14 or later.

Which languages is it in?

English, Spanish, French, German, Italian, Polish, Belarusian, Japanese and Korean. TimeRunner follows the language chosen for it in System Settings → General → Language & Region → Applications.

What does it cost?

TimeRunner is free to download, and everything is free for the first month. After that it is kept by the month, by the year, or bought once: see the plans. The prices are shown in the app and on the App Store before you buy.

What happens without a plan?

TimeRunner turns read-only: your records stay on your Mac and can still be opened, exported and backed up, but timers cannot be started and nothing can be added or changed.

I installed TimeRunner while it was free. Do I have to pay?

No. A copy installed while TimeRunner was free keeps everything for good, at no charge.

How do I restore a purchase on another Mac?

Settings → About → Restore purchases, with the same Apple Account in the App Store.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Settings → About → Manage subscription, or your App Store account settings. Cancelling takes effect at the end of the period already paid for. Refunds are handled by Apple.
